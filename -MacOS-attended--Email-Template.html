Subject: Setup Instructions for your Connection (macOS Manual Setup)

Hello,

To access the securely from your Mac, please follow these step-by-step instructions to manually create the connection in System Settings.

Step 1: Create the VPN Connection

  1. Open System Settings.
  2. Select VPN.
  3. Click on +.
  4. Select L2TP over IPsec….
  5. Change Display Name to .
  6. Server Address = .ddns.net.
  7. Account Name = .
  8. Password = .
  9. Shared Secret = vpn.
  10. Click on Create.

Step 2: How to Connect and Disconnect

To make a connection, click the toggle on.

To disconnect, toggle the connection off.

Step 3: Access the Scheduler

Once connected to the VPN, open your web browser and go to http://10.0.0.209:8090/ to access the scheduler.

A copy of the User Guide can be downloaded from NSRC Flex Cadre Manual.
