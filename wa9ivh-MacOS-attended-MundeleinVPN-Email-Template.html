Subject: Setup Instructions for your MundeleinVPN Connection (macOS Manual Setup)

Hello,

To access the MundeleinVPN securely from your Mac, please follow these step-by-step instructions to manually create the connection in System Settings.

Step 1: Create the VPN Connection

  1. Open System Settings.
  2. Select VPN.
  3. Click on +.
  4. Select L2TP over IPsec….
  5. Change Display Name to MundeleinVPN.
  6. Server Address = MundeleinVPN.ddns.net.
  7. Account Name = wa9ivh.
  8. Password = wa9ivh344.
  9. Shared Secret = vpn.
  10. Click on Create.

Step 2: How to Connect and Disconnect

To make a connection, click the toggle on.

To disconnect, toggle the connection off.

Please contact me if you encounter any authentication errors or blockages during setup.
